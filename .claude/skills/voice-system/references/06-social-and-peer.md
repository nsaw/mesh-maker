# A. CAPTIONS (Instagram, Facebook, Reddit)

## The above-the-fold rule

The first two lines are all most people see. **Line one must be complete, legible alone, and bait.** Alliteration, pun, or an open loop. Not information.

> Fails: "Eleven months, two of them arguing with a door." (Two of what? Reader hasn't committed.)
> Works: "The door won. Twice."

Nick's note: "I usually try to put an alliteration, a pun, or a hook to get users to stay on post longer and read the caption."

## Mechanics

- Emoji permitted here and in peer register only. Off everywhere else.
- Rhythm gate suspended. Captions fragment harder than VO and that is correct.
- Hedges around 20 per 1k. Higher than VO.
- The self-deprecating close is standard: "be gentle, I'm delicate 😌"

# B. PEER REGISTER (texts, DMs)

**Not a fifth surface. The source layer.** Everything above is a filtered version of this, which settles a long-open question: YouTube VO is not a performance of his voice. It is his text voice with the vulgarity dial moved and a bookend bolted on.

## The proof

The same joke, four days apart, to two different friends, at two lengths. The two-pass law caught in the wild with no camera on.

> **Compressed:** "Whoever coined sleep like a baby has never slept next to a fucking baby. Goddamn grunt ruckus"
>
> **Expanded:** "whoever coined 'sleep like a baby' has never slept next to a goddamn baby, because they make so many sounds that sound so alarming all night long: just random gasps for air, like, 'Oh shit, I haven't been breathing for a while!' it's hard to sleep through lol But overall it's pretty cool. My acceptable level of poop on things has gone from zero to a little poo is acceptable"

That is D4 aphorism cold open, plus D21 imagined voice, plus D17 flat undercut ("it's pretty cool"), fully formed, in a text message.

## Traits

- **Profanity highest here, and affectionate rather than aggressive.** It intensifies, it does not attack. "the fucking sponsor game." "all the ways to fuck things up."
- **"lol" is the defusal token.** Same job as "just kidding about the crack house, mom." It lands exactly where stakes get real: "Gonna name him Harry (the boy who lived) well, hopefully lol."
- **Quantified everything (D20).** "Worked about 2600 hours on it in the last 6 months." "$15k of fireworks (like 7+ PALLETS)."
- **Escalating question triple.** "Like what about a butthole causes organic wear?! Beach use?! Eating kitty litter?"
- **Reciprocity structure.** Answer, then hand the turn back with two or three stacked questions. Machine drafts end on a CTA instead.
- **"Pun intended"** is a real recurring tic, in texts and in scripts.

## Calibration

Bottom of the dial, for knowing where the floor is. Nothing above this register goes here.
> "Can't remember the last time I was sober today. Usually balls deep in LSD tripping my tits off right now."

## Why this matters for the other registers

When a draft in any register feels stiff, the fix is usually to write the peer version first and then filter up, rather than writing the polished version and trying to loosen it. He generates in this register and edits toward the others.
